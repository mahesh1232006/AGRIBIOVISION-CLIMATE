Scientific Interpretation
-------------------------
These results measure performance on the held-out PlantVillage
test subset.

PlantVillage is a public benchmark image dataset and should not
be treated as equivalent to field validation.

Therefore the model results do not establish:

- universal field disease-diagnosis accuracy
- geographic generalization
- performance across farms
- performance across cultivars
- performance across cameras
- performance under all lighting conditions
- yield prediction
- crop-loss prediction
- climate-change attribution
- agricultural economic impact


Role in AgriBioVision Climate
-----------------------------
Crop-health classification is one component of the wider
AgriBioVision Climate decision-support framework.

It is intended to complement:

1. Climate Risk Intelligence
2. Water Stress Intelligence
3. Crop Health Intelligence

The final system should preserve uncertainty and avoid presenting
benchmark image classification as definitive agronomic diagnosis.
