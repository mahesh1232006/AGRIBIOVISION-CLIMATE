
AGRIBIOVISION CLIMATE — PHASE 14 SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Phase 14 integrates three analytical dimensions:

1. Climate exposure
2. Water stress
3. Crop-health concern

The integrated score uses the following proposed screening framework:

Integrated Risk =
0.35 × Climate Risk
+ 0.35 × Water Stress
+ 0.30 × Crop Health Concern

The climate and water components are derived from daily meteorological
observations. Crop-health concern is scenario-based because the Phase 13
PlantVillage image observations are not temporally linked to the climate
observations.

Therefore Phase 14 does NOT claim that a specific climate observation caused
a specific disease prediction.

The framework is intended to prioritize observations and illustrate how
multiple stress dimensions can be combined transparently.

Scientific status:
- Screening-level framework: YES
- Field validation: NOT PERFORMED
- Global validation: NOT ESTABLISHED
- Climate-to-disease causal inference: NOT CLAIMED
- Climate-change attribution: NOT CLAIMED
