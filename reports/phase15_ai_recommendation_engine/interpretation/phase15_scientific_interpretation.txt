The Phase 15 engine converts the Phase 14 integrated screening framework into
transparent agricultural decision-support recommendations.

The recommendation engine is rule-based and traceable. It does not claim that
an observed climate condition caused a particular plant disease because the
Phase 13 PlantVillage image observations are not temporally linked to the
NASA POWER climate observations.

Crop-health scenario values are methodological scenario assumptions rather than
disease probabilities.

Recommendations should therefore be interpreted as screening-level guidance
for monitoring, verification, water-management review, climate-risk awareness,
and local agronomic assessment.
