INTERPRETATION
--------------
The Resource Pressure Index is a normalized decision-support indicator.

It should NOT be interpreted as:
• litres of irrigation water required
• litres of water saved
• monetary savings
• yield increase
• carbon reduction
• measured farm-level economic impact

Actual impact measurement would require farm-specific baseline data,
intervention records, water-use measurements, yield observations, costs,
and/or verified activity-based emission factors.

The climate-water compounding indicator identifies observations where climate
and water-stress signals are jointly elevated. It is a screening construct and
does not prove that one factor caused the other.

SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
----------------------
1. Phase 14 is a screening-level integrated risk framework.
2. Crop-health image observations are not temporally linked to individual
   NASA POWER climate observations.
3. PlantVillage benchmark performance does not establish field validation.
4. Global validation has not been established.
5. Resource-pressure weights are methodological design choices rather than
   statistically optimized parameters.
6. Actual farm-level impact requires prospective field measurements.

DECISION-SUPPORT POSITION
-------------------------
Phase 16 provides a transparent way to communicate potential resource and
climate pressure while avoiding unsupported claims of measured savings or
causal impact.
