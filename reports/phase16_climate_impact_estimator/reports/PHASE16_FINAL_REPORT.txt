IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
-----------------------------------
The Resource Pressure Index is a normalized screening-level indicator.

It is NOT:
• litres of water required
• litres of water saved
• monetary savings
• yield increase
• CO2 reduction
• measured farm impact

Actual impact requires farm-level intervention and outcome measurements.

SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
----------------------
• Phase 14 is a screening-level integrated framework.
• Phase 13 PlantVillage image observations are not temporally linked to
  individual NASA POWER observations.
• PlantVillage benchmark performance does not establish field validation.
• Global validation is not established.
• Phase 16 weights are methodological design choices.
• Prospective field measurements are required for real-world impact validation.

COMPETITION-SAFE CLAIM
----------------------
AgriBioVision Climate estimates normalized climate and resource-pressure
indicators to support monitoring and adaptation decisions.

It does not claim measured savings or causal agricultural outcomes without
field evidence.
