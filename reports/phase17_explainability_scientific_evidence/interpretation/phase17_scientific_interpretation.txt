====================================================================================================
AGRIBIOVISION CLIMATE — PHASE 17 SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
====================================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Phase 17 documents how project outputs can be interpreted, what evidence supports them,
what assumptions are used, and what claims must not be made.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CLIMATE EVIDENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Climate indicators are derived from meteorological/solar observations obtained through
NASA POWER and transformed through the documented AgriBioVision Climate methodology.

These observations support climate-exposure screening.

They do not constitute direct measurements from every farm.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CROP HEALTH MODEL
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The crop-health component uses a MobileNetV2 transfer-learning model evaluated on a
controlled PlantVillage-based benchmark.

Recorded Phase 13 benchmark results:

Accuracy              : 0.883086
Balanced Accuracy     : 0.882000
Macro Precision       : 0.902000
Macro Recall          : 0.882000
Macro F1              : 0.882300
Weighted F1           : 0.882900

These values describe the project benchmark.

They do NOT establish field accuracy.

They do NOT establish global accuracy.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. INTEGRATED RISK
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Integrated Risk combines:

Climate Risk
Water Stress
Crop Health Concern

according to the documented Phase 14 weighted methodology.

The score is intended for screening and prioritization.

It is not a probability of crop failure.

It is not a probability of disease.

It is not a yield-loss prediction.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CROP HEALTH SCENARIOS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Healthy baseline, moderate concern and high concern are scenario conditions.

They are not disease probabilities.

The scenario values are methodological inputs used to examine how integrated risk changes
under different crop-health conditions.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 15 recommendations are generated through transparent rules.

The recommendation engine is therefore explainable at the rule level.

A recommendation should be interpreted as a decision-support suggestion requiring
contextual/local agronomic verification.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. IMPACT INDICATORS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Phase 16 provides normalized screening indicators including:

Resource Pressure
Climate Exposure
Compounding Index
Monitoring Pressure

These indicators are NOT direct measurements of:

- litres of water saved
- money saved
- yield increase
- disease reduction
- CO2 reduction

Actual impact requires measured intervention and outcome data.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. CLIMATE-TO-DISEASE CAUSALITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The current architecture does not establish that a particular climate observation caused
a particular disease prediction.

Phase 13 image observations and Phase 14 climate observations are not temporally linked
field observations.

Therefore causal climate-to-disease claims are disabled.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. FIELD VALIDATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Field validation has NOT been performed in this project.

Future validation should include:

- independent field images
- multiple locations
- multiple cultivars
- different growth stages
- varying lighting/background conditions
- local agronomic labels
- linked climate observations
- linked intervention/outcome measurements

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. GLOBAL GENERALIZATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Global validation has NOT been established.

The project is designed to be extensible beyond one region, but extensibility is not
the same as empirical global validation.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION POLICY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The project distinguishes:

DOCUMENTED EVIDENCE
    Data or results directly supported by a source or experiment.

METHODOLOGY
    A transparent computational design used by the project.

ASSUMPTION
    A chosen threshold, weight or scenario condition.

SCREENING INDICATOR
    A normalized measure used to prioritize attention.

LIMITATION
    A known boundary on interpretation or generalization.

This distinction should be maintained in the final application, presentation and pitch.
